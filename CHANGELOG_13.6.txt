13.6 / versionCode 79

Stabilization release based on the last responsive 13.4 player layout.
- Removed network substitution of MovieAd display banners that caused JavaScript SyntaxError and advertising bootstrap retries.
- Removed persistent known-ad and manual-rule MutationObservers from the player document.
- Reduced repeated player layout/ad-rule injections during page startup and settling.
- Player layout remains bounded at the working 920x330 class of size.
- Player WebView keeps Windows 10 Chrome desktop User-Agent.
- VAST no-fill remains limited to explicit preroll descriptors; bootstrap and main media are allowed.
- Manual ad picker remains available, but saved rules are applied only at bounded lifecycle points.
